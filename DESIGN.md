# HTS Medical Laboratory — Design Context

## Creative direction
A premium clinical brand that feels calm, precise and human rather than generic "hospital blue". The supplied HTS identity is retained through cyan, deep navy and white, while the web system uses generous editorial spacing, soft glass surfaces and restrained motion.

## Audience
Patients and visitors in Ikate, Elegushi and the wider Lekki/Lagos area looking for laboratory, screening, blood banking and wellness services.

## Palette
- Deep Navy: #071C2D
- Clinical Navy: #0A2C43
- HTS Cyan: #08AFD0
- Bright Cyan: #18C9E6
- Soft Cyan: #E9FAFF
- Signal Lime: #D8EF38

## Typography
- Display/UI headings: Manrope 700–800
- Body/interface: DM Sans 400–700
- Small labels: DM Sans 700–800 with tracking

## Signature
The hero combines the supplied laboratory photography with a dark clinical overlay and a floating "Today at HTS" operating-hours card. This creates an immediately recognisable laboratory/wellness identity without reproducing the supplied flyer as a website.

## Interaction
- Scroll-triggered reveal animations.
- Glass navigation on scroll.
- Responsive mobile navigation.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Native semantic links/buttons with visible interactive affordances.

## Content source
The supplied HTS logo/flyer is the source for service names, phone numbers, email, address and opening hours.
